About BiomeXplore

Meet the team behind the innovative gut microbiome and exposome platform

Our Mission

BiomeXplore was born from the need to integrate complex gut microbiome data with environmental factors to unlock new insights in human health. We combine expertise in bioinformatics, microbiology, and data science to create transformative solutions.

We believe the key to significant health advances lies in the holistic understanding of the interaction between our microorganisms and the environment around us.

A specialist in Clinical Microbiology, she is a senior physician at the Vigo University Hospital Complex (CHUVI) and leads the Microbiology and Infectology group at the Galicia Sur Health Research Institute. Her work focuses on genomic surveillance of pathogens and microbiota innovation, co-authoring over 70 scientific papers. She actively promotes real-time clinical 'omics' in Galicia through her role at SOGAMIC and teaches Biomedical Engineering at the University of Vigo.
